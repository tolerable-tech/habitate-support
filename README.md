# Habitate Support

Welcome! This is the support hub for the **Habitate iOS App**. Whether you've run into a bug, have an idea to share, or need to get in touch privately, you're in the right place.

---

## How to get support

Support is handled through **GitHub Issues** — a free, public way to report problems and share feedback. Everything is tracked transparently so we can follow up and keep you in the loop.

> **New to GitHub?** No worries — see [Getting started with GitHub](#getting-started-with-github) below. It's free and takes less than a minute to sign up.

---

## Open a support request

Choose the option that best fits your situation:

| Type | Description | Open |
|------|-------------|------|
| 🐛 **Bug Report** | Something isn't working as expected | [Report a bug](https://github.com/tolerable-tech/habitate-support/issues/new?template=bug_report.md) |
| 💡 **Feedback & Feature Request** | Share an idea or suggestion | [Share feedback](https://github.com/tolerable-tech/habitate-support/issues/new?template=feedback.md) |
| 🔒 **Private Contact Request** | Need a confidential conversation | [Request private contact](https://github.com/tolerable-tech/habitate-support/issues/new?template=private_contact.md) |

---

## What to expect

- **Response time:** We aim to respond within a few business days. Response times may vary depending on volume.
- **Public issues:** Bug reports and feedback are public by default so other users can follow along and contribute. Please don't include personal or sensitive information in those templates.
- **Private requests:** If you need to share something confidential, use the [Private Contact Request](https://github.com/tolerable-tech/habitate-support/issues/new?template=private_contact.md) template. You don't need to share sensitive details in the issue itself — just enough for us to understand what you need.
- **No guarantees:** While we read every report and piece of feedback, we can't promise that every feature request will be implemented or every bug will be fixed on a specific timeline.

## Getting started with GitHub

GitHub is free to use and you don't need any technical experience to open a support request. Here's how to get started:

1. **Create a free account** — Visit [github.com/signup](https://github.com/signup) to register.
   - You can sign up with your **email address**, or use **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Apple** for a faster experience. See [GitHub's guide to signing up](https://docs.github.com/en/get-started/start-your-journey/creating-an-account-on-github) for step-by-step instructions.
2. **Click one of the links above** — Each link opens a pre-filled form so you know exactly what information to provide.
3. **Fill in the form and click "Submit new issue"** — That's it! You'll receive email notifications when there are updates.

If you already have a GitHub account, just click any of the links above to get started right away.
