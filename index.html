Any progress counts.

Habitate is a simple habit and goal tracker for iPhone and iPad. Check in, watch your streaks grow, and keep going even on the days life gets in the way.

{% include app-store-button.html height=54 %}

Free to download. Your data stays in your iCloud.

Built for keeping streaks alive

Daily, weekly or monthly goals, with checking in as quick as a tap.

Streaks

See every day of progress

Every goal keeps its own streak, and your overall streak shows how many days in a row you've shown up.

  • Daily goals, or a few times a week or month
  • Track an amount, like glasses of water or pages read
  • Calendars and stats for every goal
Widgets

Check in from your Home Screen

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show today's goals and your streak, and let you check in with one tap. There's a Control Center control too.

Siri & Shortcuts

Just tell Siri

Say “Log Meditate in Habitate” and you're done. Or use Shortcuts to check in automatically, for example when you close another app.

See Shortcut recipes →

Apple Health

Workouts that check themselves in

Link a goal to your workouts or mindful minutes in Apple Health, and Habitate checks it in for you. Habitate only reads from Health and never writes to it.

Streak freezes & repairs

Had a rough day? A freeze protects your streak, and a repair fixes a missed one.

Private by design

No accounts and no ads. Your goals sync through your own iCloud. Privacy policy

Habitate Plus

Unlimited goals, monthly streak freezes and repairs, and Apple Health auto-complete.
